2. **Setup**: Open Voicemod and follow the on-screen setup wizard to configure your microphone and output settings. This step is crucial for ensuring the software works correctly with your audio devices. You’ll need to select your input and output devices in the settings.

### Practical Example: Using Voicemod with Discord
1. Open Discord and navigate to User Settings > Voice & Video.
2. Set your Input Device to "Voicemod Virtual Audio Device."
3. Adjust your Output Device as needed.
4. Test your microphone to ensure the voice modulation is functioning.
